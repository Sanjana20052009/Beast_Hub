● ORIGIN & EVOLUTION

THE STORY SO FAR

From an unassuming bedroom in Greenville, North Carolina to commanding the largest digital entertainment operation on earth.

In 2012, at age 13, Jimmy Donaldson uploaded his first gaming clip to YouTube under the moniker MrBeast6000. Unlike creators who chased fleeting trends, Jimmy treated the platform as an algorithmic puzzle to be decoded. He spent day and night deconstructing retention graphs, thumbnail psychology, and storytelling pacing.

His defining breakthrough came in 2017 when he counted to 100,000 in a grueling 44-hour session. That raw human determination proved that radical dedication and unscripted endurance could captivate millions without traditional television gatekeepers.

Instead of keeping brand sponsorships, Jimmy pioneered an unprecedented financial model: reinvesting 100% of revenue into higher production values, grander sets, and life-changing giveaways. This flywheel transformed his channel from internet curiosity into an international cultural force.

📅 2018 – 2020 ♥ Philanthropy

Giving Away Money & The Reinvestment Flywheel

When Jimmy received his first significant brand sponsorship ($10,000 from Quidd), rather than keeping the income, he gave every dollar to a person experiencing homelessness. Realizing that high-stakes generosity generated far higher viewership than flashy materialism, he initiated his famous flywheel: every dollar earned went right back into bigger productions.

✨ WHY THIS MILESTONE MATTERS

Transformed creator sponsorship from personal compensation into community-giving entertainment spectacle.

$10,000 initial check • 100% video revenue reinvested

📅 November 2021 🎬 Mega Production

$456,000 Squid Game In Real Life

At a cost of over $3.5 million, Jimmy and his team built full-scale physical replicas of every set from Netflix's viral drama Squid Game, casting 456 contestants to compete in safe, non-lethal playground challenges for a $456,000 prize. The video garnered over 100 million views within just a few days.

Marked the moment online video production quality and budget parity fully matched and surpassed broadcast television.

CREATOR-LED CHARITABLE INITIATIVES

Transforming competitive creator culture into coordinated collective action tackling global ecological challenges.

🌳
EST. OCTOBER 2019

#TEAMTREES

20 Million Trees Planted

Launched alongside Mark Rober and the Arbor Day Foundation, #TeamTrees set a goal to raise $20 million ($1 = 1 tree). Tens of thousands of creators uploaded simultaneous videos with zero monetization competition, reaching the 20-million tree milestone in under 60 days. Trees have been planted across every continent except Antarctica.

Key Partners: Arbor Day Foundation, US Forest Service, international forestry organizations.
▶ Watch TeamTrees Documentary

EST. OCTOBER 2021

#TEAMSEAS

34 Million Pounds of Trash Removed

Partnering with Ocean Conservancy and The Ocean Cleanup, #TeamSeas targeted 30 million pounds of plastic waste in global oceans, rivers, and beaches ($1 = 1 pound). The initiative funded solar-powered river Interceptors that stop floating garbage before reaching open seas, alongside community cleanup operations across 60+ countries.

Key Partners: The Ocean Cleanup (Boyan Slat), Ocean Conservancy, global coastal communities.
